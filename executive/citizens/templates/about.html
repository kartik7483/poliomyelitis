About the Polio Vaccination Project

The Polio Vaccination Project is a dedicated public health initiative focused on the complete eradication of poliomyelitis (polio) through widespread immunization, education, and community engagement. Our mission is to ensure that every child, regardless of location or circumstance, receives life-saving polio vaccines and protection against this preventable disease.

Our Vision

A world completely free of polio—where every child grows up healthy, strong, and safe from the threat of paralysis caused by the poliovirus.

Our Mission

To eliminate polio through:

  • Coordinated vaccination campaigns
  • Awareness and education programs
  • Collaboration with local health workers, governments, and global partners
  • Reaching underserved and high-risk populations

Why Polio?

Polio is a highly infectious disease that primarily affects children under the age of five. Though incurable, polio can be prevented with a simple and safe vaccine. Thanks to global efforts, polio cases have been reduced by over 99%—but it still exists in a few regions. Continued vigilance is essential to prevent resurgence and protect future generations.

What We Do

  • Routine and mass immunization drives in high-risk areas
  • Outreach to remote communities to ensure no child is left behind
  • Training for health workers and volunteers on safe vaccine delivery
  • Community awareness campaigns to dispel myths and encourage participation
  • Monitoring and reporting to quickly identify and respond to outbreaks

Join Us

We believe that eradication is within reach, but it takes all of us. Whether you're a health worker, volunteer, donor, or community member—you have a role to play in ending polio forever.
