Protect Your Child from Polio

Ensure your child receives timely polio vaccinations. A healthy future starts today.

Why Polio Vaccination Matters

Polio is a life-threatening disease that affects young children. Vaccination is the only way to protect your child. Our mission is to reach every home and ensure every child gets the necessary doses on time.

What You Need to Do

  • Register your child on this portal
  • Keep track of upcoming vaccination dates
  • Visit the nearby vaccination booth during scheduled area visits
  • Encourage other parents in your area to vaccinate

"Every drop counts – your child deserves a polio-free future."
