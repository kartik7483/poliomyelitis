Every visit, every record, and every dose brings us one step closer to a polio-free world. Your dedication matters.

About the Polio Vaccination Project

The Polio Vaccination Project aims to eliminate polio through proactive community outreach and vaccination. Field workers like you are the backbone of this mission — delivering vaccines to children, tracking immunization status, and ensuring no child is left behind.

Your Role as a Field Worker

  • Visit assigned areas to vaccinate eligible children
  • Update vaccination records in the system accurately
  • Ensure timely dosage and report any follow-up requirements
  • Communicate with hospitals and supervisors for daily coordination
  • Support awareness drives within local communities
